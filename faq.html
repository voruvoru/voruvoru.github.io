Commission FAQ

VoruVoru commission questions

What can I commission?

Current options include chibi headshots, half-body illustrations and full-body character art. Creator-focused requests such as emotes or VTuber assets can also be discussed.

What information should I send?

Send a character reference or a clear written description, the artwork type you want, intended use, preferred deadline and any style or pose notes.

What is personal vs. commercial use?

Personal use covers non-monetized personal display. Monetized channels, merchandise, advertising or brand activity may require commercial usage rights and a separate quote.

What currency is used?

Commission prices on the site are listed in USD.

How do I request a commission?

Use the commission request form on the homepage. Payment integrations should only be used after the live payment configuration is enabled.
