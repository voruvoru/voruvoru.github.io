VTuber Asset Checklist: What to Prepare Before Your Debut

A VTuber debut involves more than one illustration. Preparing the visual assets in advance helps keep your channel, social profiles and stream presentation consistent.

Character reference

Start with a clear reference showing colors, hairstyle, outfit, accessories and any details that must remain consistent. This becomes the visual source of truth for future assets.

Avatar or model artwork

Choose the format that fits your setup: a static PNG avatar, reactive PNG set, or a more advanced rig-ready model. Confirm technical requirements with the artist before work begins.

Emotes and stickers

Prepare a small core set first: hype, laugh, cry, rage and love are widely reusable. Add niche reactions after you learn how your community interacts.

Stream graphics

Common items include starting-soon, be-right-back and ending screens, chat frames, alerts, panels and thumbnails. Match the color palette to the character design.

Social profile assets

Prepare profile pictures, banners and announcement graphics for the platforms you plan to use. Keep safe margins so important text or artwork is not cropped on mobile devices.

Usage rights

Before ordering, confirm whether your planned use is personal or commercial. Monetized streaming, sponsorships and merchandise may require additional rights.

Before ordering: collect reference images, usage details, preferred dimensions, deadline and examples of the mood you want.
